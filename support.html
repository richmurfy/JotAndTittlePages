Jot & Tittle — Support

Jot & Tittle is a daily Bible word puzzle. Each day has a hidden word from the Bible to find in six guesses, followed by a verse and a short devotion.

Getting in touch

Email richmurfy@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone or iPad you are using and its iOS or iPadOS version, and the date of the puzzle or the verse you were playing.

The daily word

Most days the word comes straight from the Bible's pages, four, five or six letters long. Now and then it's the name of a person or a place; if a name has you stuck, the clue hint tells you which kind of word you're looking for. Type a guess and tap return. Each tile then shows how close you came: green for the right letter in the right place, amber for a letter in the word but in the wrong place, and red for a letter that isn't in the word. When the game ends, win or lose, there's a verse with the word in it and a short devotion to go with it.

  • Hints are off to start with. The bulb gives a clue to what kind of word it is: a person, a place, a name of God or another word. The keyboard button shades the keys by the words still possible, and the eye shows suggested guesses.
  • Review: when a game ends, the review button shows how much each guess narrowed things down, beside the guess the solver would have played.
  • Share: once a game ends, share your guesses as coloured squares, with the day's verse.
  • Not sure what a button does? Touch and hold it.

Practice

The Practice tab gives you as many extra words as you like, played just like the daily word, with the same hints and review. Each word you finish comes with its verse. Practice skips the daily words for the next two months, so it won't give away a puzzle that's coming up soon.

  • Choose your words: the filter button at the top left picks words, people, places, or any mix.
  • Next word: when a game ends, the arrow button starts another. The arrow at the top right starts a new word at any time, after checking with you if you're partway through one.

Verses

The Verses tab hides two to four words from a well-known verse. Each hidden word comes with a starting guess already coloured, and you have one guess to find it: only one word fits every colour. Find them all to read the verse. Each verse opens when you finish the one before, and finished verses can be opened again from the list.

  • A typo won't cost you the word. A guess that doesn't fit the colours you've been shown isn't counted, and the line above the keyboard says why.
  • Stuck? The bulb under the word gives a hint: first another coloured guess, then a letter in its place.
  • Give up on a word? The eye shows it. It counts as missed.

History

History keeps every daily puzzle from the day you first opened the app, with your statistics and streaks. Tap a day on the calendar to look back at it, or to play a day you missed.

Common questions

When does the new word appear?
At midnight, in your iPhone's or iPad's own time zone.
I missed a day. Can I still play it?
Yes. Tap the day in History. A day played late is kept with your games but doesn't count towards your streak.
Why can't I play days before I started?
Your calendar begins on the day you first opened the app, so everyone's streak starts fresh.
Do practice games count towards my statistics?
No. Statistics and streaks count daily puzzles only. Finished practice games are listed under Recent games in History.
It says my guess isn't in the word list.
Guesses have to be words the app knows, of the same length as the answer. The guess doesn't use up a turn; try another word.
My guess in Verses wasn't counted.
It didn't fit the colours of the guesses already shown for that word. The line above the keyboard says which letter is the problem, and you still have your guess.
Which Bible translation does the app use?
The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), except for two devotions that quote the New International Version (NIV). Matthew 5:18, which gives the app its name, is from the King James Version.
Can I type with a keyboard?
Yes. With a keyboard connected to your iPad or iPhone, type the letters, press Return to enter a guess, and Backspace (the delete key on Apple keyboards) to remove a letter.
Do my games move to a new iPhone or iPad?
They're kept on the device you play on, with no account, so an iPhone and an iPad each keep their own games. They're included in the device's own backup, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone or iPad brings them across. Deleting the app deletes them.
Does the app cost anything?
No. It's free, with no ads and no purchases.

The name

“Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled.” Matthew 5:18, KJV

A jot is yod, the smallest letter of the Hebrew alphabet. A tittle is the tiny stroke of the pen that tells one letter from another. In this game, every letter counts.
