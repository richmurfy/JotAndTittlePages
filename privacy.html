Jot & Tittle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The short version

Jot & Tittle collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own. The words, verses and devotions are part of the app, and everything it keeps stays on your iPhone or iPad.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your games: each daily puzzle and practice word you play, with its guesses, and your progress through the verse puzzles.
  • Settings: which hints you've turned on, which kinds of word Practice gives you, and the day you first opened the app, where your calendar begins.

None of this is sent anywhere, and other apps can't read it. Deleting the app deletes everything. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone's or iPad's own backups (to iCloud or a computer), which belong to you; the developer can't see them.

Sharing a result

The Share button creates a short text summary of your game. It goes only where you choose to send it, through the system share sheet.

The link to Guess Grid

The About screen links to Guess Grid, another app by the same developer. If you tap it, the app asks Apple's App Store to show Guess Grid's page. That page comes from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy; nothing about you is sent to the developer.

Crash reports

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's or iPad's Settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports and usage statistics to the developer. That is controlled by your device, not the app, and can be turned off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released.
